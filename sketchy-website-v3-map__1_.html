Our Story

From a Dining Table
to Your Table

Since childhood, there was a little dream — a dream of opening a restaurant. It began at the dining table, where food was more than just meals; it was love, laughter, and togetherness.

"Food is not just to satisfy hunger — it is to create memories."

What started as a simple conversation at home suddenly turned into a plan. Our parents joined in with their blessings and encouragement. And just like that, a family dream was born.

Every step of this journey has been ours — built with our own hands, fueled by our passion. This restaurant carries the warmth of family, the flavors of passion, and the spirit of togetherness.

Welcome to our dream turned reality.
Welcome to The Sketch-y Cuisine.
