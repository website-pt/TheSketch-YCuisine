Our Story

From a Dining Table
to Your Table

Since childhood, there was a little dream — a dream of opening a restaurant. It began at the dining table, where food was more than just meals; it was love, laughter, and togetherness. I would often share this dream with my mother, not knowing that one day it would take shape so beautifully.

What started as a simple conversation at home suddenly turned into a plan. Our parents joined in with their blessings and encouragement. And just like that, a family dream was born.

Every step of this journey has been ours — built with our own hands, fueled by our passion, and guided by the belief that food is not just to satisfy hunger but to create memories.

This restaurant is more than a place to dine; it is a reflection of our love, our hard work, and our dreams stitched together. It carries the warmth of family, the flavors of passion, and the spirit of togetherness that started at our very own dining table.

Welcome to our dream turned reality.
Welcome to The Sketch-Y Cuisine.
